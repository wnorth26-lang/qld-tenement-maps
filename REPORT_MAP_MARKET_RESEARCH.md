# Australian statutory-report map product research

Research date: 17 August 2026. This is a product specification, not regulatory,
legal, geological or environmental advice. The current title instrument,
reporting period, activities undertaken and regulator guidance control the
actual map set for each submission.

## Product conclusion

The customer journey should be `jurisdiction → report/workflow → map product`,
not a technical renderer dropdown. A report may require several maps, and
activity-specific maps are conditional on work actually performed.

| Jurisdiction | Regulator-supported core | Product implication |
|---|---|---|
| QLD | Relinquishment/final reports require a technical summary including maps and digital geochemical/geophysical data. | Location/context, surrendered-retained area, life-of-title work and conditional activity maps. Keep the existing official sub-block plan. |
| NSW | Reports must contain maps/plans/data necessary to interpret the report; partial/final reports require a clear map of all exploration over the authority life. | Period activity, geology and conditional drill/sample/geophysics maps; life-of-title map for partial/final. |
| VIC | The technical report structure expressly includes an exploration index map; guidance also calls for a geology map, activity maps and topographic disturbance plans. | Make exploration index and geology first-class annual products, then conditional drill/sample/geophysics/disturbance products. |
| WA | Annual/surrender reports use a tenement location map; surrender must distinguish surrendered and retained land. Maps require scale bar, GDA2020 datum and coordinate axes. | Tenement location and surrender-retained maps are core; geology and activity products follow work performed. |
| SA | MG13 covers annual activity summaries, technical and partial surrender reports and national digital-data standards. | Use the common annual/partial-surrender set, but mark individual products conditional until MG13 content is encoded and tested. |
| TAS | Annual reports expressly include a topographic activity-summary map; final/partial reports require surrendered/retained mapping and life-of-licence evidence. | Activity summary is the lead annual product; surrender-retained and life-of-title maps lead final products. |
| NT | Regulations require maps locating exploration, survey boundaries, drilling and geological samples relative to title boundaries. | Annual map pack should explicitly offer activity index, surveys, drilling and samples rather than one generic location map. |

## Common commercial map catalogue

1. Tenement location and regional context.
2. Exploration activity index.
3. Regional and prospect geology.
4. Drilling and sample locations.
5. Geophysical/remote-sensing survey extents and results.
6. Disturbance and rehabilitation.
7. Surrendered versus retained area.
8. Life-of-title exploration activity.
9. Proposed work program.
10. Environmental, land and overlapping-tenure context.

The site must label each product as regulator-explicit, conditional on activity,
commercial context, or not yet renderable. It must never generate a QLD map for
another jurisdiction merely because that is the only available adapter.

## Competitor positioning

- Hetherington publicly offers preparation/lodgement of statutory reports and
  broader tenement, environmental and due-diligence consulting. Its proposition
  is managed professional service rather than instant self-service cartography.
- Measured Group combines technical/annual/final reporting, GIS/cartography,
  environmental services and tenement management.
- LandTracker provides Australia-wide current-tenement mapping, project/group
  views, satellite/topographic/geology/geophysics context, alerts and custom
  compliance reporting.
- 1slate focuses on the tenement system of record, obligations, work programs,
  expenditure and configurable reports.

Agent Evidence Labs should therefore not compete as another generic map viewer
or calendar. The differentiator is a regulator-aware map-pack builder that:

- selects the evidence maps appropriate to the state and report;
- queries official sources and records verified-zero/source-failure states;
- creates consistent report-ready artifacts and manifests;
- passes maps directly into the evidence/report workflow; and
- leaves geological interpretation and professional sign-off with the client or
  qualified reviewer.
